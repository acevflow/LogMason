# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

LogMason is an open-source project intended to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in its development and community.

We are committed to making participation a positive experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or personal circumstances.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors and participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Welcome constructive feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Focus on technical issues and ideas rather than personal criticism.
- Provide actionable feedback and engage in good-faith discussions.
- Respect the time, effort, and contributions of others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional, collaborative environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions carried out on behalf of LogMason.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately through an appropriate contact method listed in their GitHub profiles.

Please provide enough information to help the maintainers understand the situation. Reports will be handled as carefully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing reported violations. Depending on the circumstances, they may issue a warning, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.

Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Changes

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the project and its community evolve.
